# Apple Pencil and gestures

The Apple Pencil always draws; fingers move the canvas.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Action |
|---|---|
| Pinch | Zoom |
| Two fingers drag | Pan |
| Two fingers twist | Rotate |
| Two-finger tap | Undo |
| Three-finger tap | Redo |

A finger stroke that turns into one of these gestures is cancelled and leaves no mark. The **Fit** button in the top bar (or ⌘0) brings the canvas back.

While you move it, the canvas **clicks into place** with a light tap you can feel: upright and at every quarter turn (90°, 180°, 270°), at its fitted size, and in the middle. Keep turning or dragging to go past.

## Only Apple Pencil draws

In the brush options, **Only Apple Pencil draws** stops fingers from drawing at all; they still zoom, pan and undo. It starts the way the iPad's own "Only draw with Apple Pencil" setting is.

## Double tap and squeeze

Double-tapping the Apple Pencil (or squeezing the Pencil Pro) does what you chose in **Settings → Apple Pencil**:

- **Switch to eraser**: eraser, and back to the tool you had.
- **Switch to previous tool**.
- **Show colour palette**: opens the colours.

## Pressure

**Pencil pressure changes size** in the brush options makes the line thinner when you press softly. Turn it off for an even line, the usual choice for pixel art.
