# Preferences

Open them with the **gear** in the gallery, **Preferences…** in the file menu, or ⌘,.

![Preferences](/crops/en/preferences.jpg)

## Language

Calco is in **English** unless you choose otherwise, whatever your iPad's language. Pick **Español** to switch at once, without restarting. Your projects and the names of layers and clips don't change.

## View

- **Grid around the canvas**: a faint grid behind the canvas, like a cutting mat. On by default.
- **Show drawing latency**: milliseconds from the Pencil to the screen, in the top bar, for checking performance.

## Other settings

Tool settings are remembered on their own, outside Preferences: each brush's size and opacity, the eraser's size and tip, colour, smoothing, pressure, fill gaps and where the paint goes. **Pixel art** belongs to each project.
