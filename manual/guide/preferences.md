# Preferences

Open them with the **gear** in the gallery, **Preferences…** in the file menu, or ⌘,.

![Preferences](/crops/en/preferences.jpg)

## Language

Calco is in **English** unless you choose otherwise, whatever your iPad's language. Pick **Español** to switch at once, without restarting. Your projects and the names of layers and clips don't change.

## Other settings

Tool settings are remembered on their own, outside Preferences: brush and eraser sizes, colour, smoothing, pressure, fill gaps and where the paint goes. **Pixel art** belongs to each project.
